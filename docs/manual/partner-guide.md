# Getting your organization on the GNBYA Activities Guide

The **GNBYA Activities Guide** (https://if-phillip-break.github.io/gnbya-activities-app/)
helps Greater New Bedford families find camps, programs and organizations for
their kids. Every listing comes from partners like you, and it's free.

All three forms are on one page:
**https://if-phillip-break.github.io/gnbya-activities-app/partners.html**

## 1. Add your organization (once)

Use **"Add or update your organization"** if you're new to the guide.

- Tell families who you serve: ages, grades, services, cost, schedule, where
  you are, and a short description.
- **Website:** give the full address, starting with `https://`.
- **Listing manager email:** use an inbox your organization will keep. It's
  private, never shown on the site, and it's where your links go.

GNBYA staff review new profiles before they appear. You'll get an email straight
away with a **private link** to update your profile later. Keep it.

**Already listed?** Don't fill the form in again, because that makes a second
copy. Use your private link instead (see 3).

## 2. Add an activity

Use **"Add an activity"** for each program, camp or class. Give each one its own
form if it has different ages, cost or dates.

- **Organization Name:** pick yours from the list. If you're not there, choose
  **Other** and type your name.
- **Activity Type:** *Current Activities* for the school year, *Summer
  Activities* for summer.
- **Session Dates:** write both ends of every session, for example
  "July 6 - Aug 14" or "Tuesdays 9/29-11/17". Families can search by date, and
  the site reads your dates.
- A few extra questions may appear depending on your answers, such as cost and
  translation.

Staff review it before it appears, and you'll get an email with its private
link.

## 3. Update a listing

Open the **private link** from your email, change your answers, and click
**Submit**. Once your listing is approved, changes appear on the site within
about five minutes, with no need to wait for staff.

- **Not running this season?** Set *Listing Status* to "Not running this
  season". Families still see it, marked as not running.
- **Taking it down?** Choose "Remove from site" (*Listing Status* for an
  activity, *Organization status* for your organization). It disappears right
  away. Note that removing your **organization** also hides all its activities.

Each link opens one listing and lets anyone who has it make changes, so please
don't share it.

## 4. Lost your links?

Use **"Lost your edit links?"** and enter your listing manager email. If it
matches, we'll email you every link you manage within a few minutes. Check spam
if it doesn't arrive.

No email? Your listing may have been added by GNBYA staff without an email on
file. Write to **lead@gnbya.org** and we'll send your links.
