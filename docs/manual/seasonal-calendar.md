# Seasonal calendar

The guide has two kinds of activity: **Current Activities** (school year) and
**Summer Activities**. The website changes some things by itself through the
year; this is what staff should do around that.

## What the site does by itself

| Months | Summer Activities tab |
|---|---|
| **March to August** | Normal. Cards say **Sign up →** |
| **September to February** | Turns into an archive: the tab is labelled **Past**, a notice says "Summer *year* · finished", every card is greyed and says **Visit website →** instead of Sign up |

The switch happens automatically on 1 March and 1 September. **Nothing is
hidden either way**: summer listings stay published all year so families can
see what an organization usually offers.

All year, any activity whose dates have clearly passed is greyed, marked
**Session ended**, and moved to the bottom of its tab. The site **assumes every
date is in the current year.** A listing written as "January to June" reads as
*this* January to June, so late in the year it shows as ended even if it runs
again next year. Teen Ambassador (The Zeiterion) is in this state now.

## Month by month

**Every month**
- Check the Activities tab for yellow rows you haven't approved yet.
- On the website's **Current Activities** tab, look at greyed "Session ended"
  cards. For each one, ask the partner to update its dates through their link.
  If it's over for good, untick **Show on Site** or ask them to choose "Remove
  from site".

**January**
- Ask partners with spring programs (February break, April break, spring term)
  to add or update them as Current Activities.
- Fix year-spanning listings that now read as ended, for example by asking the
  partner to write the new dates.

**February: invite summer submissions**
- Email every partner the partners page
  (https://if-phillip-break.github.io/gnbya-activities-app/partners.html):
  add new summer programs, and update last summer's using their private links.
- Remind them to change dates, cost and ages. On **1 March the Summer tab goes
  live again with whatever is in the sheet**, so last summer's listings will
  show as current until partners update them.
- Send links by hand to anyone without an email on file (see the staff guide).
- **Most summer listings were typed in by hand and have no edit link.** Ask
  those partners to submit this year's programs as new activities through the
  form. As each one is approved, untick the old hand-typed row (see "Replacing
  an old hand-typed listing" in the staff guide).

**March to May**
- Approve summer submissions quickly: most families plan summer in the spring.
- By the end of May, check every summer listing has this year's dates. Untick
  any that won't run, or ask the partner to set "Not running this season".

**June to August**
- Watch for "Listing edited" emails as camps fill up or change.
- Late August: ask partners for fall and school-year programs (Current
  Activities).

**September**
- The Summer tab archives itself. Nothing to do for summer.
- Approve fall programs. Check the Current Activities tab isn't left with only
  ended listings.

**October to December**
- Mid-season check of Current Activities: ended fall sessions, winter programs
  coming up.
- December: ask for winter and January programs.

## Once a year

- Check the Organizations tab with partners: closed organizations, new
  websites, changed ages. Each organization can do its own through its link.
- Send each organization its link if it still has no listing manager email.
